A top-down action RPG. Pick a class, clear three maps and fight through to the end.

Made with

AR / VR

Two solo projects built with A-Frame. They run in a browser, so there is nothing to install.

Solar System AR

A markerless AR solar system for the classroom. Fly between the Sun and eight planets over your camera view and read the facts in Thai.

Made with
A-Frame, AR.js
Team
Solo project
  • Nine 3D models, each spinning, with an animated camera flight between them
  • Zoom, pause and a Thai info panel for every planet
  • Teacher mode adds extra notes and pauses the spin while you explain

Allow camera access when asked. Open it on a phone or a laptop with a webcam.

Open live demo Source code

Digimon Digital World VR

A WebXR scene where you enter the Digital World, evolve Digimon at three stations and fuse WarGreymon and MetalGarurumon into Omegamon.

Made with
A-Frame, WebXR
Team
Solo project
  • Intro sequence with a typed terminal, a gate and a wormhole tunnel
  • Fourteen 3D models, a cutscene for every evolution and audio controls
  • Custom components for auto-fitting models, the lab boundary, stations and fusion

A fan tribute made for coursework. The characters, music and clips belong to their owners.
